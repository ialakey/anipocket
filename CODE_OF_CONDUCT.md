# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## The longer version

This is a small project with a narrow purpose. Things that are welcome here:
bug reports with enough detail to act on, fixes for parsers that sites have
broken, questions when the documentation is unclear, and disagreement about
technical decisions.

Things that are not: harassment of any kind, personal attacks, demeaning or
discriminatory remarks, publishing anyone's private information, and sustained
disruption of discussion. Insisting that someone do unpaid work for you is also
not welcome — nobody here owes you a fix.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, commits, issues and pull requests
that do not follow this, and may block accounts for repeated or severe behaviour.

## Reporting

Contact [@ialakey](https://github.com/ialakey) directly. Reports stay private,
and I will not take action against someone for reporting something in good faith.
